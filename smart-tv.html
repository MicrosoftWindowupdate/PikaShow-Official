Yes! The official app has a built-in screencast feature. You can easily cast movies from your mobile directly to any Chromecast-enabled Smart TV without installing the app on the TV.

Is the app completely free on Smart TV?

Yes, the pika show experience is 100% free regardless of the device. There are no premium subscriptions or hidden charges for TV users.

Does it work on Samsung (Tizen) or LG (webOS) TVs?

PikaShow is an Android application. It does not install directly on non-Android systems like Tizen or webOS. For those TVs, you can either use the Cast feature from your phone or connect an Amazon Firestick.
